# **Bit 0001: Summer/Early Fall Ships**

Here’s what our hackers have been building since last school year!

- ✦ [Sketchi](https://sketchi.ing/) — an online imposter drawing game
- ✦ [sieve-ai-image-detector](https://x.com/sriramkiron/status/2089385332189941901)— an AI image blocking extension that won an award
- ✦ [Toggle Complex](https://mcthouacbb.itch.io/toggle-complex) — a puzzle platforming game about flipping switches to open and close gates
- ✦ [Lexeme](https://github.com/SrGaabriel/lexeme) — a command line tool that searches dictionaries across 400+ languages to help you name your projects
- ✦ [Integer Overflow Endgame](https://www.usenix.org/conference/usenixsecurity26/presentation/zhang-zheng) — an academic security research paper that received a Distinguished Paper Award at USENIX Security '26
- ✦ [Harry's Line Cam](https://harrysline.com/) — a live camera monitor using computer vision to track wait times and food truck presence
- ✦ [Purdue Hackers Webring Redesign](https://ring.purduehackers.com/) — our webring directory redesigned with sound design and live website previews
- ✦ [SHA256 Gradient Randomart](https://www.shadertoy.com/view/N3tGRS) — a GLSL shader generating procedural gradient artwork from the first 8 bytes of a SHA-256 hash
- ✦ [Life of a Dropdown](https://dropdown.kobra.systems/) — an interactive retrospective recreating 17 historical dropdown menu interfaces from 1983 to 2026
- ✦ [Oura Ring CLI](https://github.com/rayhanadev/oura) — a CLI tool and background daemon for private, local-first Oura Ring data syncing over Bluetooth
- ✦ [firewall](https://github.com/Ethan-Ka/firewall) — custom snooper board hardware and firmware for Purdue Flight Display E32 units
- ✦ [Live Fit Gym Apple Wallet Pass](https://x.com/_jack_hogan/status/2095917689990688895) — a reverse-engineered Apple Wallet pass replacing a gym's proprietary rotating QR code app
- ✦ [Rekord Ripper](https://github.com/ImTheSquid/rekord-ripper) — a command line utility to transfer Rekordbox analysis data across songs, and find better copies of your tracks
- ✦ [Spill≋ Album](https://www.youtube.com/playlist?list=PLG6rpqxg5_LM) — a smooth jazz album produced in FL Studio that premiered at the Purdue Hackers Fall Showcase

Want to see more ships? Check out: [https://ships.purduehackers.com/](https://ships.purduehackers.com/)

For even more, visit our discord! ([https://phack.rs/discord](https://phack.rs/discord))

# **Bit 0011: Our Webring**

First, what’s a webring? A webring is a group of separate sites linked together, usually a hobby group or friend group’s sites. This was popular during the early days of the internet and personal homepages (like on GeoCities).

Of course, we at Purdue Hackers have our own webring (at [https://ring.purduehackers.com/](https://ring.purduehackers.com/)) and 5 people recently joined us with their new sites!
